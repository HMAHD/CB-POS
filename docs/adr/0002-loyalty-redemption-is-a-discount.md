# Loyalty redemption is a discount, not a payment method

Redeemed points reduce the bill like a bill discount (at the configured Point Value), instead of appearing as a "Loyalty" payment line. Shelf prices are MRP with tax inside, so a discount lowers the MRP total that VAT and SSCL are back-calculated from, which matches how the shop actually gave value away; a payment method would leave tax on the full amount and make reports, the cash drawer rule and refunds treat points like money. Points are earned on the Final Bill after redemption, so redeemed rupees never earn points.
